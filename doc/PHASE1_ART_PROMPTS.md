# Pixel art da Fase 1

Gerado com a ferramenta integrada imagegen; sprites importados com transparência, filtro Point e sem compressão/mipmaps. Os recortes respeitam os limites visuais das linhas geradas (não uma grade regular perfeita). Assets: Assets/Art/Environment/Phase1/indoor.png e outdoor.png.

## Prompt exterior

Create ONE production game asset: a square 4 by 4 sprite atlas (16 equal square cells) on truly transparent background for a Brazilian folklore 2D SIDE VIEW platformer, Lendas do Quintal. Crisp authentic 16-bit pixel art, limited earthy palette, dark navy outlines, warm amber accents, no smooth painting, no gradients, no labels or text, no characters. Every item separate and centered in its own cell with at least 12% transparent margin; nothing crosses cell boundaries. Same pixel scale throughout. All objects strictly SIDE VIEW profile/elevation, never isometric. Row 1 left to right: wooden chicken coop with wire mesh and straw, old stone well with timber roof and rope bucket, rustic wooden fence segment, thick leafy backyard tree with horizontal climbable branch. Row 2: old wheelbarrow filled with pumpkins, pile of firewood and chopping stump, washline strung with small cloth garments, flowering terracotta pots cluster. Row 3: tall banana plant, gnarled forest tree with hanging vines, mossy rock cluster, dense fern and shrub cluster. Row 4: wooden bench, old bicycle leaning by a post, watering can and seed sack, broken wooden cart. Cozy countryside at night, readable silhouettes. Objects have their feet/base at the same baseline within each cell. Alpha background, no ground plane or cast shadows outside objects. This is an asset atlas for cutting into 16 independent sprites.

## Prompt interior

Create ONE square 4 by 4 sprite atlas (16 equal square cells) with a truly transparent background for a cozy Brazilian grandmother's countryside house in a 2D side-scrolling game. Authentic crisp 16-bit pixel art, dark navy outlines, warm terracotta wood, parchment cream, midnight blue shadows, amber lamplight, small distinct hard pixel clusters, no smooth painting, no text, no isometric view. Keep every prop separated in its cell with 12% transparent margins, matched pixel density. Straight side-view/elevation so they sit naturally behind a side-view walking hero. Row 1 left to right: small wooden bed with blue quilt, bedside cabinet with oil lamp, tall wooden wardrobe, open toy chest with wooden toys. Row 2: vintage upholstered armchair, tall crowded bookcase, grandmother's rocking chair with knitted shawl, low wooden coffee table with radio. Row 3: old wood-burning iron stove and pot, ceramic kitchen sink in wooden stand, rustic pantry cabinet with jars, wooden dining table with bread and ceramic bowls. Row 4: hanging brass oil lantern, wooden arched door, square wooden window with blue curtains and amber light, cluster of framed family pictures. Warm family home atmosphere, sturdy readable silhouettes. Objects grounded to consistent baseline inside each cell. Genuine alpha transparency, no floor planes or external cast shadows, no characters. This sheet will be sliced into sixteen standalone sprites.
